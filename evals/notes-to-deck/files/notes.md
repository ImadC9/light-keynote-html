# Supplier onboarding — lunch & learn (notes)

Audience: ~25 buyers and AP colleagues, 20 minutes.

## Why we're talking about this
- New supplier requests take 11 working days on average from request to "active in ERP" (Jan–Sep). Target is 5.
- 38% of requests come back at least once because something is missing (bank letter, tax certificate, contact email).
- Every rework loop adds ~3 days.

## How the process works today
1. Buyer raises request in the portal
2. Supplier fills the onboarding form
3. Master data team checks documents
4. Compliance screening (sanctions, ESG questionnaire if spend > 50k)
5. Finance validates bank details (call-back)
6. Supplier active

## Where it breaks
- Buyers send the form before confirming the supplier's legal entity name → mismatch with tax certificate
- Bank letter not signed / not on letterhead
- ESG questionnaire triggered late because expected spend isn't filled in
- Nobody owns chasing the supplier

## What we're changing
- Pre-check list for buyers before raising the request (legal name, tax ID, expected annual spend, contact)
- Portal now blocks submission without expected spend
- Master data sends one consolidated "missing items" email instead of several
- Buyer owns supplier chasing for the first 5 days, then master data takes over

## Ask to the audience
- Use the pre-check list from Monday
- Tell suppliers upfront: signed bank letter on letterhead
- Feedback channel: #supplier-onboarding

Close with: "Five days is possible if the request is right the first time."
